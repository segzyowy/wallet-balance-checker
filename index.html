THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Minimal disclosure ≠ zero knowledge.

This app reads a balance; it does not generate a zkTLS or zero-knowledge proof. Ethereum addresses and transactions are public. Connecting exposes your address to this app and its wallet provider. An exported result is a self-reported summary—not something a service should trust for access, credit or rewards.

01 / CHECK

One question, one snapshot.

Is the balance at least the threshold? Live mode reads the latest block and queries native ETH at that block hash. A snapshot can become outdated or be affected by a chain reorganization.

02 / MINIMIZE

No history requested.

No transaction history, token approvals or private keys. Wallet details stay in page memory; downloads are saved only when you choose. No analytics or third-party scripts.

03 / KNOW THE LIMIT

A real proof needs more.

A production system needs a reviewed proof protocol, authenticated chain state, ownership binding, freshness and replay protection. This independent lab is not a Primus integration.

Live wallet setup & troubleshooting

Use an Ethereum-compatible browser wallet on Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1) or Sepolia (11155111). An embedded preview may not expose browser extensions; open the app in a normal browser tab or serve these files on localhost. If the RPC provider does not support block-hash balance queries, this app reports an error rather than silently weakening the snapshot check. Sepolia ETH is test currency and has no monetary value. “Disconnect” clears this page’s account state; revoke site permissions in your wallet separately.
